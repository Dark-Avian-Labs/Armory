## SemVer (baseline **1.0.0**)

Existing package/release numbers are ignored for this backfill.

- `feat` → **minor** (patch resets to 0)
- `fix`, `perf`, `revert` → **patch**

- `ci` alone → **patch** (same as chore for this simulation)
